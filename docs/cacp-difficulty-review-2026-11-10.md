# CACP bank difficulty review (2026-11-10)

This file lists every reworked unofficial practice item so a clinician can check the keyed answer, the source, and why the item is harder. It is educational practice material, not a dosing order and not an AC Forum examination.

## Why the previous bank was easy

- 211 of 219 stems were short recall prompts. About 8 were long enough to look like vignettes.
- 146 of 219 correct answers were choice B (25 A, 27 C, 21 D).
- The correct choice was uniquely the longest in 207 of 219 items, so length was an answer cue.
- Distractors were often short and implausible (a mechanism that does not exist, or a one-line absurdity next to a paragraph of the right idea).
- Stems often asked for the textbook sentence directly.

## What changed

- All 219 items were rewritten in place. Ids, domainId, objectiveIds, and related links are unchanged.
- Correct-answer letters are balanced within each domain (overall A 56, B 55, C 54, D 54). The exam still shuffles display order.
- Stems are cases: age, weight, serum creatinine, interacting drugs, INR trend, procedure bleed risk, or a clinic failure are in the story, including details that do not decide the question.
- Each explanation says why the keyed choice is right and why each other choice is wrong, and names the label, guideline, or trial.
- Choice length is closer than the old bank. The old keyed choice was uniquely longest in 207 of 219 items (mean gap about 60 characters; 146 items were more than 40 characters longer than the next choice). After this rewrite those figures are 177 of 219, a mean gap of about 19 characters, and 39 items still more than 40 characters longer. Length is a weaker cue. It is not gone.
